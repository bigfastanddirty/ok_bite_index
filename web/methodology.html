Algorithm Weighting & Limnological Hierarchy

Weight: 30% Max +/- 18 pts

1. Barometric Rate of Change (ΔP 3h)

Fish swim bladders are direct hydrostatic pressure sensors. Rather than scoring static pressure, the algorithm evaluates the 3-hour smoothed trend (ΔP/Δt):

  • • ΔP ≤ -1.8 hPa: Strong pre-frontal feeding surge (+18 pts)
  • • ΔP -0.8 to +0.4 hPa: Steady / stable pressure (+4 pts)
  • • ΔP ≥ +1.5 hPa: Post-frontal lockjaw / bottom staging (-16 pts)
Weight: 25% Max -10 to +16 pts

2. Dam Generation & Hydrodynamics

USACE dam releases create predatory current breaks along main-lake points, bridges, and secondary creek channels:

  • • Release > 6,000 CFS: Heavy ambush current (+16 pts)
  • • Release 1,500 - 6,000 CFS: Active feeding current (+10 pts)
  • • 0 CFS & Pool Drawdown: Stagnant / falling pool (-10 pts)
Weight: 20% Max -14 to +12 pts

3. Water Temperature & Metabolism

Evaluates seasonal metabolic efficiency and spawn phases across warm-water and cool-water species:

  • • 62°F - 76°F: Peak metabolic feeding & spawn (+12 pts)
  • • 54°F - 62°F: Pre-spawn staging (+6 pts)
  • • > 88°F or < 45°F: Thermal stress / winter torpor (-14 pts)
Weight: 15% Max -10 to +10 pts

4. Light Attenuation & Wind Chop

Predator fish lack adjustable irises. Moderate wind chop and dense cloud cover break up surface light:

  • • Overcast ≥ 70% & Wind 7-16 mph: Roaming predators (+11 pts)
  • • Bluebird Sky & 0 mph Wind: Spooky / buried in cover (-7 pts)
  • • Wind > 25 mph: Dangerous / blown out (-8 pts)

Estimated Water Temperature & Limnological Thermal Decay Model

Not every USACE reservoir or USGS gauge station maintains an active in-situ sub-surface thermistor year-round. When physical temperature telemetry is offline, OK Bite Index activates an automated Thermal Decay & Thermal Inertia Model:

1. Exponential Moving Average (EMA)

Calculates a 7-to-10-day weighted moving average of ambient air temperatures to capture lake thermal lag and heat capacity.

2. Solar Radiation & Inflow Modifiers

Factors shortwave solar radiation (W/m²), cloud cover opacity, and major cold runoff inflows from upstream tributaries.

3. Reservoir Mean Depth Damping

Applies reservoir-specific thermal damping coefficients based on mean pool depth (e.g., deep lakes like Tenkiller vs. shallow lakes like Canton).

*Whenever physical telemetry is reporting live, the estimated model is automatically bypassed in favor of raw sensor readings.

Dissolved Oxygen (O₂) Saturation & Limnological Estimation

Most freshwater reservoirs lack continuous electrochemical optical dissolved oxygen probes in shallow surface bays. When in-situ USGS DO telemetry is offline, OK Bite Index calculates theoretical surface DO saturation (mg/L) derived from the Henry's Law water-temperature solubility relationship:

1. Temperature-Dependent Solubility

Cold water holds substantially higher dissolved oxygen concentrations (DO ≈ 14.6 - 0.1 × Water Temp °F). As surface water warms above 80°F, saturation thresholds naturally decay toward 6.0–7.0 mg/L.

2. Wind Aeration & Inflow Dynamics

Surface mechanical agitation from sustained wind (>8 mph) and high-volume spillway/tailrace flows increase atmospheric re-aeration across upper epilimnion layers.

3. Hypolimnetic Anoxia Suppression

Identifies summer thermal stratification cutoff points (thermoclines) where deep bottom basins drop below 3.0 mg/L, concentrating active predators into shallow oxygen-rich margins.

*When active USGS optical DO sensors report live telemetry (e.g., Lake Arcadia or Skiatook), estimated values are bypassed and flagged as raw physical readings.
